Sector Focus & Value Creation

The Markets We Target

FnB focuses on sectors where diaspora investors can combine commercial value with tangible local economic impact. Every project passes strict internal screening and verification before being presented as suitable for consideration.

SECTOR A

Building & Construction

Key Opportunity Profiles:

  • Residential housing, apartments, housing estates and student accommodation.
  • Commercial buildings, retail centres, and mixed-use real estate developments.
  • Schools, education infrastructure, and healthcare/senior-living facilities.
  • Hotels, serviced apartments, tourism accommodation, and co-working offices.

Market rationale: Official investment materials highlight a continuing need for housing, commercial structures, and offices, backed by strong urban demand growth across Dodoma and other regional hubs.

SECTOR B

Agriculture & Agribusiness

Key Opportunity Profiles:

  • Avocado and horticulture value chains.
  • Oil palm, sunflower, and edible-oil processing lines.
  • Soybean and mixed-crop farming operations.
  • Agro-processing, storage, packaging, irrigation, and agricultural distribution inputs.

Market rationale: Tanzania positions agriculture as a primary investment pillar, with TIC regularly issuing targeted sector guides and curated land/project opportunities.

SECTOR C

Blue Economy

Key Opportunity Profiles:

  • Commercial fisheries, fish processing, cold-chain, ice plants, and storage facilities.
  • Aquaculture, cage/pond fish farming, feeds, and hatcheries.
  • Water transport, boat services, dry docking, and lake logistics infrastructure.
  • Lake and coastal eco-tourism, recreation, and aquatic ecosystem restoration services.

Market rationale: We establish the Blue Economy as a distinctive category encompassing aquatic value chains, fisheries infrastructure, and sustainable lake/coastal development.
