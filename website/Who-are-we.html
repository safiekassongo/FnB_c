About FnB

Who We Are

FnB is a trusted, multi-disciplinary consulting firm offering a broad range of investment advisory to diaspora investors interested in real estate, energy, infrastructure development, agriculture and agribusiness. We provide solutions to both public and private clients across the board. From midsize commercial developments, to master planned communities, to local building projects, FnB delivers outstanding project results, builds long-lasting relationships and leverages the growth of our organization to serve the constantly changing needs of our clients.

As a result of methodical corporate planning and diligent implementation, our dynamic leadership has focused on strategic expansion that has resulted in a national project portfolio and opportunities to increase our geographic presence for both new and existing clients. FnB offers on-demand technical genius and industry-leading talent that, when combined, produce innovative and effective results

Our Core Mission

Clarity and trust in cross-border capital

Navigating investments from abroad comes with unique friction points. Our goal is to replace uncertainty with institutional-grade transparency, rigorous research, and on-the-ground accountability.
