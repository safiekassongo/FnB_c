What does a diaspora investor actually receive?

Turning distance into a manageable process

Distance can create information gaps, weak accountability, and limited visibility. Our service model converts distance into accountability through evidence, verification, reporting, and structured project oversight.

SERVICE 01

Secure Digital Client Evidence Portal

What the investor gets:

  • A secure digital space for project documents, photographs, inspection records, contracts, approvals, and reports.
  • Time-stamped evidence and an organized project record.
  • Centralized communication and document access.
  • Progress visibility without relying exclusively on family members, contractors, or informal intermediaries.
  • A searchable audit trail of key project decisions and evidence.

Investor outcome: Greater visibility, easier record keeping, and a stronger evidence base for decisions.

SERVICE 02

Investment Research & Opportunity Screening

What the investor gets:

  • Initial market and sector assessment.
  • Location-level opportunity assessment.
  • Project screening against defined criteria.
  • Preliminary commercial logic, demand, and competitive considerations.
  • Identification of key assumptions, red flags, and information gaps.
  • A concise screening report to support a go/no-go decision.

Investor outcome: Less time spent chasing unsuitable opportunities and better-informed investment choices.

SERVICE 03

Due Diligence & Verification

What the investor gets:

  • Structured verification of promoter/project identity and documentation.
  • Site and asset verification where applicable.
  • Checks on project status, ownership claims, and supporting records.
  • Verification of key assumptions against available evidence.
  • Risk register highlighting unresolved issues.
  • An evidence pack showing what was verified, what was not verified, and what requires specialist advice.

Investor outcome: Reduced information asymmetry and clearer visibility of project risks before committing further capital.

SERVICE 04

Legal & Title Verification

What the investor gets:

  • Coordination of land/title and corporate-document checks with qualified local professionals.
  • Verification of critical documentation and transaction parties.
  • Identification of missing approvals, encumbrances, or inconsistencies where discoverable.
  • A clear list of legal questions requiring resolution.
  • Referral to licensed lawyers, surveyors, valuers, or other regulated professionals when specialist opinions are required.

Investor outcome: Better protection against preventable documentation and ownership risks.

At FnB we facilitate and coordinate specialist legal work; however, we do not replace a licensed legal practitioner.

SERVICE 05

Project Management & Monitoring

What the investor gets:

  • Defined milestones, deliverables, and responsibilities.
  • Scheduled site inspections and progress checks.
  • Photographic and documentary evidence of progress.
  • Early warning on delays, cost pressures, and scope changes.
  • Progress reports against agreed milestones.
  • Escalation of material issues to the investor.

Investor outcome: Confidence that someone is physically checking what is happening on the ground.

SERVICE 06

Procurement Oversight

What the investor gets:

  • Support in defining procurement requirements and specifications.
  • Comparison of quotations and supplier information.
  • Basic supplier verification and conflict-of-interest checks where feasible.
  • Purchase documentation and delivery evidence.
  • Checks that purchased goods/services align with approved requirements.
  • Exception reporting where actual procurement differs materially from plan.

Investor outcome: Greater transparency over how project money is converted into materials, equipment, and services.

SERVICE 08

Training, Webinars & Corporate Partnerships

What the investor gets:

  • Investor education on project evaluation and risk awareness.
  • Webinars on sectors, locations, and market conditions.
  • Access to selected professional and institutional partners.
  • Knowledge-sharing sessions for diaspora investors and project promoters.
  • Corporate partnership opportunities that can strengthen project execution and local value chains.

Investor outcome: Better investment literacy, stronger networks, and improved access to relevant expertise.
