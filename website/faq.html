Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how we institutionalize trust, protect capital, and manage remote investments on your behalf.

Q1 What is FnB and who do you serve?

FnB (Forward in Business) is an investment advisory and project oversight institution built primarily to serve diaspora investors. We bridge the distance gap by providing independent research, stringent due diligence, title verification, and active on-the-ground project monitoring so you can invest securely without relying solely on informal intermediaries or family members.

Q2 How does FnB protect my investment from a distance?

We replace informal assumptions with verifiable structures. Every project goes through a clear roadmap: strict initial research, verified document checks, milestone-based approvals, and regular physical site inspections. You receive time-stamped evidence, reports, and real-time updates directly via your secure Digital Client Portal.

Q3 Do you handle legal and title verifications directly?

While we coordinate and facilitate background checks, land registry reviews, and corporate documentation, we operate alongside licensed legal practitioners, surveyors, and valuers. We ensure you have all the facts and risk assessments before you commit funds, and we guide you to licensed experts for final formal legal execution.

Q4 What is the Digital Client Portal?

The client portal is your secure, centralized dashboard. It houses all your project files, contracts, inspection logs, financial tracking records, and high-resolution progress photographs. It gives you absolute transparency and an audit trail of every decision point without needing to chase people over phone calls or messaging apps.

Q5 How do I start a project or request an evaluation?

You can begin by submitting an inquiry through our Contact page. Our team will review your objectives during the initial Enquiry & Onboarding phase, define the mandate and scope, and establish a clear plan before any capital or major commitments are made.

Q6 Where do you currently operate?

We have active regional coordination hubs and local field teams operating across East Africa (including Tanzania and neighboring markets). Visit our Where We Operate page to view our regional presence and operational networks.

Have a specific question not answered here?

Reach out to our team directly and let’s discuss your unique project requirements.
