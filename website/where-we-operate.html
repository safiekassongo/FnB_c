Regional Investment-Support Network

Where We Operate

FnB is building a regional investment-support network across selected growth centres in Tanzania, Burundi and Eastern DRC. Our location strategy combines established commercial centres, administrative capitals, tourism corridors, agricultural zones and lake-based economies.

MACRO CONTEXT

Diversity of Regional Investment Activity

Tanzania Investment Centre (TIC) data show strong regional investment activity, with 901 registered investment projects in 2024 representing about US$9.31 billion in capital. Dar es Salaam accounted for 356 projects and about US$4.44 billion, Arusha 64 projects and about US$213.06 million, Dodoma 47 projects and about US$182.36 million, Mwanza 37 projects and about US$581.11 million, and Kigoma 8 projects and about US$155.62 million.

HUB 01

Dar es Salaam | Tanzania

Role in FnB network: Tanzania's principal commercial and investment hub, with strong relevance for property, construction, logistics, trade, services, tourism, manufacturing and coastal/blue-economy activities.

Key Focus Sectors:

  • Residential and commercial property, apartments, mixed-use developments and office accommodation.
  • Retail, warehousing, logistics and commercial infrastructure.
  • Hospitality and tourism-related projects.
  • Coastal and blue-economy opportunities, fisheries, and marine services.
  • Special Economic Zones and export-oriented manufacturing.

Why it matters: Offers market depth, business connectivity, and priorities identified by TIC around modern markets, shipping, and tourism.

HUB 02

Arusha | Tanzania

Role in FnB network: Northern Tanzania's tourism, horticulture, services and commercial gateway.

Key Focus Sectors:

  • Tourism accommodation, hospitality and complementary services.
  • Residential apartments, offices and commercial developments.
  • Horticulture, high-value agriculture and agro-processing.
  • Tourism-linked retail, recreation and specialized service businesses.

Why it matters: Highlighted by TIC for tourism, industrial production, and horticulture. Kimandolu opportunity areas show high demand for commercial and residential spaces.

HUB 03

Dodoma | Tanzania

Role in FnB network: National capital and a growing administrative, institutional, commercial and real-estate market.

Key Focus Sectors:

  • Residential apartments, housing estates and serviced accommodation.
  • Hotels, conference facilities and commercial complexes.
  • Education, research, health and professional services.
  • Food processing, light manufacturing, and logistics corridors.

Why it matters: Investment opportunities cluster around Nala, Msalato Airport, Ihumwa Dry Port, and Government City developments.

HUB 04

Mwanza | Tanzania

Role in FnB network: Lake Victoria commercial centre with strong links to fisheries, trade, transport, agriculture and urban development.

Key Focus Sectors:

  • Fish processing, cold-chain, storage and fisheries value addition.
  • Aquaculture, fish-farming inputs, feeds, and hatcheries.
  • Residential and commercial property serving a growing urban economy.
  • Agro-processing, regional trade, and lake tourism.

Why it matters: High registered project capital combined with prime potential in cold rooms, cage culture, and regional logistics.

HUB 05

Kigoma | Tanzania

Role in FnB network: Strategic Lake Tanganyika and western Tanzania corridor with agriculture, fisheries, cross-border trade and tourism potential.

Key Focus Sectors:

  • Oil palm, soybean and mixed-crop agriculture and agro-processing.
  • Fisheries, aquaculture, cold-chain and fish value addition.
  • Lake Tanganyika tourism, recreation and urban support property.

Why it matters: Features major agricultural land allocations like the 14,000-hectare Uvinza soybean project, plus rich blue-economy dimensions.

HUB 06

Bujumbura | Burundi

Role in FnB network: Burundi's principal commercial city and an important Lake Tanganyika gateway.

Key Focus Sectors:

  • Residential and commercial real estate, hotels and business hospitality.
  • Lake Tanganyika tourism, recreation, and waterfront development.
  • Horticulture, food processing, cold-chain, logistics and manufacturing.

Investor note: Foreign investment compliance requires minimum thresholds (US$500,000 in Bujumbura; US$250,000 elsewhere). FnB verifies legal positions prior to client presentation.

FRONTIER MARKET

Eastern DRC | Selected Growth Corridors

Role in FnB network: A high-potential frontier market where rigorous due diligence addresses complex project, legal, security and execution realities.

Key Focus Sectors:

  • Agriculture and agribusiness value chains.
  • Real estate, construction, energy and environmental projects.
  • Health, education, technology and logistics services.

FnB principle: We never treat Eastern DRC as a single homogeneous market; every corridor is assessed independently for execution risk.
